THE WHOLE STORY

A buyer needs chairs.
What happens next?

Five steps. No tricky words.

  1. 1
    IN THE PLAN

    See the chairs.

    The website shows pictures and facts. The buyer picks a chair to ask about.

    The website is like a showroom.
  2. 2
    FORM IS READY

    Ask for a price.

    The buyer sends a request. Now the team needs to keep it in a safe place.

    No request should get lost.
  3. 3
    BUILD NEXT

    Agree on the details.

    Everyone agrees on the chair, color, sample, quantity, price, and packing.

    Everyone uses the same notebook.
  4. 4
    CONNECT NEXT

    Make and send them.

    The factory makes, checks, packs, and ships the chairs. The buyer can see what is happening.

    No chasing for updates.
  5. 5
    ADD LATER

    Help. Then sell again.

    A quick scan opens care, parts, or warranty help. Happy buyers can order again.

    The chair still has help after delivery.

Sometimes the team says hello first. That is okay. If someone says “no thank you,” the team stops.

WHAT TO BUILD FIRST

Three helpful things.

The small names are for the grown-up work plan.

1

One product notebook.

Put every chair picture, color, size, document, and packing fact in one place.

WORK NAME: PIM + DAM
2

One customer and price notebook.

Keep every request, person, sample, price, and next job together.

WORK NAME: CRM + QUOTATION
3

One order tracker.

Show when the chairs are being made, checked, packed, and shipped.

WORK NAME: ERP + ORDER TRACKER

Locks are important too. Only the right people should see each notebook. Keep backups and write down who changed something.

WHAT CAN WAIT

First make the facts right.
Then add the fancy things.

A fun chair designer

Nice later. First, the colors and options must be correct.

A big customer login

Useful after quotes and orders are working properly.

A smarter robot helper

It should answer only from approved facts.

HOW WE KNOW IT WORKS

Three easy tests.

  • 1

    Change a chair fact once.
    It changes everywhere.

  • 2

    A buyer asks for 300 chairs.
    The team sees the same request and price.

  • 3

    The buyer asks, “Where are my chairs?”
    The answer is already on the screen.

THE ONE THING TO REMEMBER

Show the chair.
Write down the request.
Make and deliver it.
Help after it arrives.
