RECOMMENDED SEQUENCE

Three horizons. Each with an exit test.

Timing is indicative and should be confirmed after systems, data quality, and internal ownership are assessed.

0–90 DAYS

Create the source of truth

  • PIM/DAM for models, options, documents, and revisions
  • CRM persistence, ownership, consent, and RFQ source tracking
  • Production email authentication and synchronized suppression
  • Identity, roles, audit, analytics, and backup foundations
Exit test

One approved model update reaches the website, quote template, employee view, and NFC destination without re-keying.

3–6 MONTHS

Connect quote to order

  • Export CPQ with currency, MOQ, packaging, tooling, and approvals
  • Sample requests, revisions, shipment, feedback, and sign-off
  • Invite-only buyer workspace for quotes, approvals, and documents
  • ERP-backed production, inspection, and shipment milestones
Exit test

A buyer and employee see the same approved quotation and current order milestone without emailing a spreadsheet.

6–12+ MONTHS

Add service and differentiation

  • NFC-enabled warranty, parts, care, and product history
  • Localized content for the highest-value buyer markets
  • Guided configuration; add CAD/BIM for A&D channels
  • Verified material, footprint, and product-passport readiness
Exit test

A scan can resolve the exact model and service context, while every public specification remains governed and current.

PRIORITIZE

What makes export buying easier

Accurate options, MOQ and packaging logic, sample approvals, compliance scope, shipment milestones, and reorder continuity.

ADD SELECTIVELY

What depends on the channel

CAD/BIM for designers, rich visualization for configurable collections, and EPD/LCA work where verified customer or market demand exists.

DO NOT FAKE

What requires real operational data

Live inventory, lead times, order status, certificate validity, AI-generated specifications, or sustainability claims without a governed source.
